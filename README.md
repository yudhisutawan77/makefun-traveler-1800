Entry for the [make.fun bounty "One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb): one sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800.

## The entry

> Imagine every library, post office, and marketplace in the world joined by one road, so any letter arrives in a blink and anyone may read it or answer back.

**156 characters** — one sentence, within the 1–200 character gate.

## Why this sentence

The judge brief weighs, most important first: does it explain the internet to someone from 1800, does it build from what they already know, is it true, does every word earn its place, and does it leave one memorable image.

- **Built from 1800 knowledge only:** a library, a post office, a marketplace, a road, a letter — no word coined after the traveler's time.
- **What it is and what it does:** the world's stores of knowledge, mail, and trade joined by a single road; any message arrives in a blink, and anyone may read it or answer back.
- **One image to remember a week later:** every library, post office, and marketplace in the world joined by one road.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`, checking first that it is exactly one sentence of 1–200 characters, and exits non-zero if it ever falls outside the gate.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
